Title: Workweave — trace a changed decision across company work

Workweave gives employees one workspace for tasks, meetings, notes, knowledge, and the people connected to their work. FalkorDB connects employees, tasks, services, approved decisions, original documents, training and reviewers. It finds the starter guide that contradicts a replacement decision, preserves the evidence behind a saved plan, and flags the plan when the decision changes. A stored review request is allowed only after the current prerequisites are met.

The demo includes a one-task employee flow, a live graph walkthrough, query evidence and a reproducible eight-case diagnostic. OpenAI reads graph context and selects a small supported A2UI layout. The UI uses official shadcn components.
